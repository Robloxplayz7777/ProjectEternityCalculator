XP formula: floor((1500 + 400n + 40n²) × 3.4), n = level − 1. It was derived by measuring the max XP of every blacksmithing level from 1 to 25 in-game and fitting a formula to those values; it matches all of them exactly. Levels above 25 (and theoretical mode) are extrapolated. Max level 35 (2,640,304 total XP). Crafts use 4 items at a time.
